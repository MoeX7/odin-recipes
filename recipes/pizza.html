Pizza Recipe

Description

A simple and a easy way to make a good looking and tasty pizza at home!
By following these steps and ingredients, lets start with what we need to make it.

ingredients

  • Dough
  • sauce
  • cheese
  • mushrooms(optional)

Steps

  1. we roll the Dough and make sure its even and round or how you prefer it.
  2. We add a layer of sauce on the Dough.
  3. And of course the cheese comes after we added the sauce.
  4. lastly we add the rest of the ingredients and spread them evenly.
  5. We slide it into the oven and wait.
